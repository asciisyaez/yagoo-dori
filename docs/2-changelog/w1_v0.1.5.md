## Changes

- **Team calculator — required Member locks**: lock up to five exact owned
  cards; locks survive exact enumeration, bounded search, refinement, and
  replacement analysis (request schema v4→v5, result v4→v6 across the
  release; multi-anchor native-search constraints). Locked cards are badged,
  excluded from stand-in suggestions, and guarded against Oshi capacity and
  duplicate-talent conflicts. Roster storage migrates v2→v3 with a
  sanitizing lock list.
- **Team calculator — selection evidence**: a "Why each Member is here"
  field report per slot (timing, links, one-for-one replacement checks with
  paired per-chart impact), a shared expand-all control, swap impact reports
  with recipient-level effect changes, and search-claim rendering.
- **Team calculator — search quality upgrade** (measurement-driven):
  request seeding with a provable floor (a legal seed lower-bounds the
  returned value; per-seed evaluations echoed and contract-reconciled), a
  deterministic effort knob (standard | thorough, default thorough), and a
  multi-start ascent with joint leader+member moves, component-guided to
  stay within budget, under a never-below-best-seen invariant (the answer
  is the argmax over everything the run fully evaluated). Every thorough
  run replays a protected standard lane, so more search can never return
  less. Fixture evidence: 102 measured drop-one violations → 3 (named,
  0.14–0.57%, deferred to the next search ticket's gate), 11 Oshi
  inversions → 0, 15 budget regressions → 0. Timings: thorough p50 ~6.1s,
  observed worst ~12s on large rosters — user-approved budget decision;
  standard ~2–3s.
- **Guides**: Sakura Miko "Radiant Beach Shot" and Hoshimachi Suisei
  "Water Gun Arpeggio" published (7 → 9 guides, 41 exact rating timelines,
  3 recorded-unavailable charts behind the upstream Cloudflare challenge —
  re-verified still unreachable on release day). New unavailable-comparison
  evidence model renders honest aggregate-only panels with no placement
  claims.
- **Game-fidelity audit corrections** (four independent audits vs the
  game's own pinned tables; three MAJORs were all claim/framing, zero were
  computation): the guide library no longer presents singer-matched charts
  as the rating universe and now states the real Holomem Score Rating rule
  (top 3 song scores as Leader across all rating-eligible songs, highest
  difficulty); "Mobile" dropped from benchmark labels (the model prices no
  platform distinction); the methodology page discloses the equal-note-
  weighting gap (game pays hold ticks 0.1×, flicks 1.05×, combo bonus to
  +10% — named the largest unpriced fidelity limitation), the
  generation-vs-attribute condition-rate partition (median 23% vs 82%
  in-condition under the frozen rotation, zero overlap — independently
  reproduced 115/115), stacking as a modelling choice, the frozen-cutoff
  qualifier, and free-Live-play scope; the calculator explains its range in
  place and links the methodology page; runtime-rule grades corrected
  (board-point-budget downgraded to corroborated; achievement point income
  reworded per in-game help — points come from Holomem Rank, achievements
  yield materials); activation-probability reading and Member Upgrade
  Bonus neutralization recorded as graded rules.
