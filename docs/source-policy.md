# Source and evidence policy

Yagoo-dori normalizes durable public hololive Dreams material into a versioned research dataset. Public structured repositories are valid inputs when pinned to a commit or retrieval timestamp; official materials and independent references are used to verify important relationships and mechanics.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Official hololive Dreams, QualiArts, and COVER materials.
2. Pinned structured public datasets, including HolodoriDB English and Japanese snapshots.
3. Independent game references such as AppMedia and Game8.

Independent editorial rankings are comparison material only. They never determine Yagoo-dori tiers or team recommendations.

## Record contract

Generated datasets record the upstream URL, version or commit, retrieval date, and transformation. Conflicting numerical claims enter the review queue instead of being silently merged.

The public model must distinguish Talent, Member card, and Leader Outfit identities and preserve actual rarity, attribute, generation, stats, progression, skill role, target condition, formation order, and legal-team constraints.

## Collection boundaries

Contributors must not bypass scrape protection, access private APIs, extract or decrypt the game client, automate an account, or use installed game files. Production UI uses local images rather than hotlinks. The backstage asset manifest records each file's source, retrieval date, and linked card or talent.

## Publication checks

A release must pass data, asset, type, lint, unit, build, static-export, and browser checks. Calculated claims remain bounded by the mechanics and chart evidence implemented in the current methodology; unresolved runtime rules cannot be replaced with invented precision.
