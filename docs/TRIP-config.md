## Planning: Technical Considerations

- **Pattern Usage**: Follow ARCHI.md — TypeScript `native-utility.ts` is the
  semantic authority; accelerators prove exact parity before trust; data flows
  sync → generate → validate → compile-in.
- **Determinism & Evidence**: New or changed computations produce versioned
  JSON evidence artifacts in `data/native/` with a `schemaVersion` and a stable
  digest that excludes timestamps/timing. Plans name the artifact, its
  generator script, and its npm script.
- **Exact Arithmetic Discipline**: Six-decimal signed integer micro-unit
  comparison only; no epsilon/tolerance may establish parity, ties, pruning,
  or optimality. Outward binary64 enclosures; ambiguity falls back, never
  guesses; equality is never pruned.
- **Claim Boundaries**: Nothing is labeled exact/optimal/certified/proof
  unless its gate passed. `certificateEligible=false` and
  `fullRunAuthorized=false` until every declared gate passes. Claim A
  (aggregate team+Leader) and Claim B (conditional 120-order) stay separate.
- **v0.1 / v0.2 Separation**: Optimizer research must not change public
  calculator claims, rankings, guides, or product UI. Rankings/guides
  regenerate only on methodology/input changes, never because an accelerator
  is parity-identical.
- **Performance Gates**: Performance work is measured (p50/p95 on normalized
  workloads) against declared targets; missed targets are recorded as failed,
  never hidden by easier corpora or combined unrelated speedups. Full-run
  gates: ≤800 raw core-hours and ≤72 p95 wall-hours with 25% contingency.
- **Parity Cost Awareness**: `optimizer:parity:bulk` and
  `optimizer:parity:compiled` each take ~11 minutes; plans state which parity
  runs a change obligates (rehash-only vs full re-execution).
- **Edge Cases**: Non-finite values, subnormals, signed zero, canonical-bucket
  boundaries, tie/finalist promotion, unsupported mechanics (must fall back
  loudly, never silently).

  `exact-optimizer-bulk-accumulation.ts`, or `exact-optimizer-arithmetic.ts`
  obligates: affected unit tests, then full parity re-execution
  (`pnpm optimizer:parity:bulk`, `pnpm optimizer:parity:compiled` — ~11 min

  resulting diff; determinism claims cite stable digests, never timestamps.
