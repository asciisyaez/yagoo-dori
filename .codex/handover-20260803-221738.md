### X06 implemented but not complete

- `packages/core/src/exact-optimizer-bulk-accumulation.ts` implements a guarded bulk accumulator for repeated equal binary64 contributions.
- The semantic reference remains ordered IEEE-754 binary64 addition: `s(i+1) = RN-even-binary64(s(i) + x)` in source order.
- The fast path constructs an outward enclosure; it may return a canonical micro-unit only when every possible result in the complete propagated enclosure maps to one canonical six-decimal micro-unit.
- Ambiguous or unsupported cases fall back to ordered replay at the affected component. Stable reasons include canonical-boundary overlap, non-finite values, subnormal assumptions, interval overflow, contribution mismatch, unsupported paths, signed-zero sensitivity, and invalid multiplicity.

- `evaluateExactOptimizerTeamLeaderCentral` is a central-only B2 entrypoint. It can prune only a certified strict central loss. Equality, finalists, and fallbacks promote to B3; B2 never fabricates lower/upper values.
- The uncompressed and ordered-state evaluators remain available as independent reference/cross-check paths.
- Stable parity evidence excludes timestamps and timing data from its authoritative digest.
