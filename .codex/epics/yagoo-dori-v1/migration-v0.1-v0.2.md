# v0.1 to v0.2 milestone migration

## Why the historical status stopped at 64%

The original weighted graph treated the native rubric, tier list, guides,
calculator, and release as one dependency chain under an unfinished unrestricted
full-roster optimizer. The project-status script therefore awarded zero weight
to completed N03D, N05, N06B, N06C, and N07 work even though the public product,
its bounded-search disclosures, and the v0.1 Pages deployment had been verified.
That conservative score reflected an invalid dependency graph, not missing v0.1
product work.

## Correction

The v0.1 epic now credits the independently verifiable product scope:

- pinned data, evidence-linked mechanics, and the deterministic native evaluator;
- the native ranking rubric and frozen tier snapshots;
- the combined database, guides, and owned-roster/Oshi/Bloom calculator;
- responsive QA and the already-live, guarded GitHub Pages release.

The calculator remains exact only for scopes it exhaustively enumerates and
bounded for larger scopes. Guides retain their declared search scope. No public
claim was widened and no scientific acceptance criterion was silently checked.

## Open v0.2 work

Scalable exact certification is a separate epic under
`.codex/epics/yagoo-dori-v0.2-exact/`. It must first freeze the complete input
tuple and objective, then pass canonical arithmetic, TypeScript/compiled parity,
measured formulation comparison, deterministic sharding, independent
verification, and scope-safe product integration. Until those criteria pass,
`certificateEligible` remains false for the full-roster run.
