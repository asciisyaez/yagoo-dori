# N06B: Optimizer-backed team guides

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each guide names an exact anchor card, Leader/Outfit, five legal Member slots, and formation order.
- [ ] Premium, standard, and viable 4-star-accessible formations come from the optimizer rather than editorial copying.
- [ ] Alternatives show replacement loss, affected recipients, timing, investment order, and the reason for the trade.
- [ ] Song-specific variants and duration breakpoints are included whenever they change the optimum.
- [ ] Calculations are useful and reproducible while unsupported precision remains absent.
- [ ] The first complete guide is independently checked against evidence and a separate reduced search.
